# Robin animation design: build and review target

Design input only. Based on the landed CCR-4 robin-perch.webp, Christmas raster brief, RobinArt.svelte, robin.ts and existing decoded-alpha validator. No generation, repository edits or validation performed in this design turn.

## Visual and coordinate contract
Generate a complete right-facing bird for EACH unique frame, with Codex image generation. Always include the shipped robin-perch.webp as identity reference, the fixed coordinate guide, and the existing Halloween finish references. Additional approved adjacent frames may help continuity but never replace the identity reference. Do not generate a sheet and cut invented frames out of it. Shared rest poses may reuse one accepted generated frame by frame ID; do not claim an existing asset was newly generated.

Keep untrimmed 128x112 cells, virtual foot anchor (64,100), .35 scale, whole-assembly mirroring, existing outer box and all route/interaction logic. Complete bird frames replace the rotating separate wing; do not rotate, interpolate or crossfade artwork. Spatial texture filtering at .35 is allowed: discrete refers to temporal frame selection, not nearest-neighbour raster scaling.

The model already translates the anchor and adds its parabola. Artwork must not add a jump arc or translate the whole bird upward in the cell. Airborne feet tuck UP relative to the same virtual anchor; never align tucked toes back onto the ground, which would move the torso down. Pin pelvis/torso landmarks to the rest registration. Source guide contains cell border, anchor crosshair, y100 ground, reference torso/head bounds and the hard envelope; output must contain no guide markings or ground line.

Every nonzero-alpha pixel, including texture-filter support and both facing directions, must fit x[-24,+24], y[-37,0] relative to feet. Preserve transparent cell margins. No painting below the floor, expanding route envelopes, clipping wings to fit, new decorations or shadow outside the bird.

## Frame plan
Times below are positive per-frame display durations. Shared endpoints between different one-shots are intentional; loop closing duplicates are forbidden.

Idle breath: four generated poses I0 neutral, I1 slight inhale, I2 full inhale, I3 easing outward. Timeline I0 I1 I2 I3 I2 I1, 200ms each, 1200ms period. This is a ping-pong sequence without repeated endpoints. Breast contour excursion <=2 native pixels, head/feet stable. I0 is the universal resting/reduced-motion still.

Blink variation: I0 open (60), B1 half lid (40), B2 closed (70), B1 reopening (40), I0 open (90): 300ms, non-looping. Keep eye size/highlight and head registration. Idle tilt: I0 (100), T1 slight curious tilt (120), T2 maximum 6-degree tilt (180), T1 return (120), I0 (100): 620ms, non-looping. Tilt about the neck, not whole bird. Choose one variation after 4-8 seconds of uninterrupted rest, alternate blink and tilt, and start at an idle neutral boundary; cancel if movement or alert starts. Use an independent injected cosmetic random stream so movement RNG, routes and rest timings remain unchanged.

Hop: seven displayed phases H0 neutral 10%, H1 shallow crouch 15%, H2 spring/extended legs 10%, H3 tucked ascent 20%, H4 tucked descent/feet preparing 20%, H5 landing flex 15%, H6 neutral 10%. H0 and H6 share I0. Five new unique poses. One-shot nominal 280ms = 28,42,28,56,56,42,28; scale the entire timeline to actual existing duration D=220-320ms. Local crouch lowers torso at most 3px, spring raises it at most 2px; these are articulation, not a second arc. H3/H4 torso position remains registered. Route begins immediately, so H1 is a brief compressing silhouette already departing the floor, not a stationary anticipation promise. Do not delay or retime the route to make it more literal.

Flight take-off: F0 neutral, F1 compress/folded wing, F2 opening wing and toe release, F3 high wing/upstroke endpoint. Four frames, equal positive durations, total .20D (90-150ms).
Flight loop: eight DISTINCT phases W0 high wing, W1 downward diagonal, W2 horizontal downstroke, W3 low wing, W4 low turnaround with feathers flexing, W5 horizontal recovery with narrowed feathers, W6 upward diagonal, W7 nearly high recovery. 25ms each, 200ms period, no copy of W0 at the end, no ping-pong downstroke. Eight separately generated frames including W0; take-off's last phase reuses W0.
Flight landing: L0 high wing approach, L1 braking/fanning with legs extending, L2 folding and feet reaching, L3 landing flex, L4 neutral. Five displayed frames, equal positive durations, total .20D; L0 reuses W0 and L4 reuses I0. All wings above ground and within cell/envelope.

Flight middle uses .60D (270-450ms). Fit a whole number of cycles N=max(1,round(.60D/200ms)); derive each frame duration .60D/(8N). Thus shortest routes have one slower flap and longest routes two quicker flaps; every loop ends at the same high-wing phase entering L0. Never append W0 or double its duration at wrapping. It is an intentional design compromise to fit existing short flights: route progress, not a fixed real-time bird physiology, drives take-off and landing. Landing flex occurs just before route completion; do not add a post-route settle delay. Nominal manifest loop stays 200ms; runtime effective timings are derived and testable.

Alert: A0 neutral (40ms), A1 eye/neck attentive (60), A2 modest head turn toward viewer/cursor (80), A3 settled attentive angle (100): 280ms one-shot; hold A3 while existing alert is true, including its existing 3s timeout. Maximum head rotation 10 degrees, <=3px head-centre translation, chest/feet unchanged, no new oversized eye or taller body. Whole-bird facing continues to come from the existing cursor-facing rule; do not introduce cursor tracking or independent wing/head mirroring. Return A3 A2 A1 I0, 60ms each, only while resting. Repeated cursor events while already alert do not restart the turn; facing reversal mirrors the current frame. Escape pre-empts alert immediately.

Target approximately 30 unique generated frames, with shared rest/high-wing IDs rather than separate duplicate assets. The exact arithmetic is 4 idle +2 blink +2 tilt +5 hop +2 take-off +8 flight +3 landing +3 alert =29 unique frames; allow rejection/regeneration attempts beyond that. Previews cover idle, blink, tilt, hop, take-off, flight loop, landing, alert entry and alert return.

## Interruption and accessibility
Priority: layout/preference/lifecycle reconciliation, movement, alert, idle variation, breathing. Cosmetic clips cannot keep or start movement.
Movement wins immediately at its existing timestamp. Do not finish a cosmetic clip first. Alert starts at A0; return blends by discrete poses only. At route completion select I0 and restart idle epoch; no lingering flight frame. Invalid route/perch, resize relocation, hidden document, theme teardown and reduced-motion change clear animation epochs alongside existing reconciliation. Resume selects the existing reconciled rest/hidden result, without replaying missed hops or alerts. Reduced motion displays accepted I0 only, with no breath, blink, tilt, turn, flap or one-shot. Preserve existing reduced-motion interaction behavior.

## Cleanup and acceptance thresholds
These are proposed engineering starting tolerances, not published standards or measured acceptance of generated frames. Production research must document the perceptual basis and calibrate against CCR-4 and rendered lab examples; do not present these numbers as already researched. Hard geometry is non-negotiable.

Record manual source landmarks and planned pose landmarks per frame: virtual ground anchor, pelvis, torso centre, head centre and shoulder. A deterministic uniform scale plus translation registers the guide. No anisotropic warp, per-frame autocrop/recentring or aligning airborne toes. Derive body scale from torso excluding head, wings, tail and legs. Report raw and cleaned registration and correction parameters; regeneration is required if registration needs distortion or hides drift.
After cleanup: anchor error <=1 native pixel (.35 CSS px); torso linear dimensions within 3% of reference for stable poses and 5% for planned crouch/compression; torso centre within 1.5px of the specified pose target. Reference-relative bounds changes and pose-target residuals must BOTH be reported so intentional articulation is visible rather than mistaken for scale error. Head width/height within 3%, except documented perspective foreshortening <=5%; no changing species, beak length, breast patch identity, contour weight or light direction. Crouch/spring/tilt/turn allowance follows the frame plan above. Wings/tucked feet may change silhouette substantially; compare their pose targets and hard envelope, not total alpha area as a scale proxy. A wing changing total bounds is not permission for torso growth.

Use flat saturated cyan #00FFFF as initial key: absent from the reference's russet/umber/ivory/dark palette and less likely than green to overlap brown/olive shading. Record actual sampled background colour, not only requested hex. Request no backdrop gradient, shadows, texture or lighting spill. Flood-fill background-connected key regions and use a controlled soft alpha ramp in a colour-distance band, then remove cyan matte from partially covered edge colours. Record thresholds and inspect disconnected holes. Reject inconsistent background or unrecoverable colour contamination; do not erode feet, eye glints or fine feather tips to hide a fringe. Key/decontaminate at full resolution BEFORE premultiplied resampling to 128x112; retain fractional alpha and convert back to straight sRGB RGBA.

Extract a reference palette of representative material/shade colours, preserving smooth warm gradients; use material-aware continuous colour mapping rather than hard nearest-colour posterisation. Compare opaque interior colours in perceptual Lab using CIEDE2000, against reference material/shade distributions, not spatially identical pixels across poses. Proposed post-map median deltaE00 <=3, 95th percentile <=6; report pre-map values and correction magnitude too. If a material needs >6 deltaE00 median correction, regenerate instead of recolouring a different character into compliance. Edge colours are inspected separately with alpha weighting; matching a palette cannot prove identity.

At .35 size, inspect dark dashboard, white and checkerboard backgrounds at DPR1 and DPR2 and mirrored facings. Reject cyan rim, dark halo, jagged contour, vanishing toes, eye flicker, breast colour pumping, sudden body-size changes and abrupt wing-root seams. Magnified nearest-pixel views diagnose problems but do not substitute for rendered-size acceptance. Mark numerical-pass/visual-pending separately. No silent tolerance relaxation; record rejected originals/reports and regenerate.

## Reviewable production and playback
Two review units on the same task:
1. Artwork/pipeline: retained untouched generator originals, exact prompts/refinements, input guide/reference copies and hashes, actual generator identity/date/attempt IDs, source landmarks, cleanup config/script and pinned dependencies, raw/cleaned measurements and rejection reasons, accepted RGBA frames, packing script and manifest, labelled contact sheets and preview GIFs.
2. Playback: minimal manifest loader/selector and RobinArt integration, cosmetic epochs without movement-policy changes, pure timing tests, decoded-art/geometry tests, Vite-only fixtures/evidence, regenerated embedded assets.
The >30-file inventory is justified by individual frame provenance. Group originals and accepted frames by animation, keep production evidence outside the embed tree, and include a compact review index identifying every attempt as accepted/rejected. Do not split runtime from its required assets into an unusable landing. No arbitrary file cap, new tree lights, spiders, API or daemon work.

Pack accepted frames losslessly into WebP sheets with pinned cwebp 1.6.0 settings from CCR-4; no trimming, rotation or palette quantisation. Manifest has version, sheet URLs/dimensions/hashes, frame IDs and absolute native-pixel x/y/w/h rectangles (128x112), common anchor, scale, ordered frame IDs, positive per-frame milliseconds, loop flag and named terminal/rest frame. Transparent gutters prevent atlas filtering bleed; exclude gutters from rectangles. Packer is deterministic; rerun and compare hashes. Reuse IDs across clips. Select one rectangle at a time; never CSS-interpolate sprite coordinates or wing angle.

Timing: half-open frame intervals, elapsed modulo total duration for loops, last frame at exact/after one-shot end plus explicit completion signal. Consumers transition to still/next clip once, even after large time jumps. Tests must cover every boundary, wrap just before/at/after period, positive finite durations, no duplicate loop closure or repeated ping-pong endpoints, ordinary seam duration, one-shot completion exactly once, skipped frames, flight D=450/750 and hop D=220/320, alert retrigger/direction/escape, preference/background/layout interruptions, independent cosmetic RNG and unchanged movement results.

Validate decoded sheet/frame dimensions, alpha and filtered bounds after mirroring, palette/landmark reports, actual DOM frame rendering, no residual raised-wing rotation, existing geometry/click-through/cursor/reduced-motion behavior. Extend Vite lab fixtures with manual timeline/frame selection, reference overlay and per-animation playback. Use /lab/artwork.html and /lab/scene.html, npm run dev and playwright.lab.config.ts only; prevent lab API access. Run go vet ./..., go test ./..., svelte-check, relevant frontend/artwork tests and make dashboard; prove new tests fail under relevant mutations and verify bundle freshness. Record actual commands/statuses, hashes, viewport/browser/DPR/time/facing and screenshot paths. No checks have been run in this design turn. Owner visual acceptance remains after landing.
